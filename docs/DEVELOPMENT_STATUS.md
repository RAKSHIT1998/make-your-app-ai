## Known issues / limitations
- Plan allowances, yearly prices and credit-pack prices are proposals (BUSINESS_MODEL.md). Validate them with real generation costs.
- No proration. Invoice numbers come from one global sequence (GST may need per-FY numbering and a GSTIN invoice PDF). No dunning emails. Platform refunds are issued in the provider dashboard and synced by webhook (see below); a subscription refund never cancels the plan.
- Legal pages fill in company details from `NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_*` and show a draft banner until all are set. The wording still needs counsel review, especially the refund commitment.
- Generation executes in the instance that accepts the request (lease-based crash recovery, no separate queue). Replicating the API needs `RATE_LIMIT_STORE=postgres` and a shared `STORAGE_DIR` (RUNBOOK → Scaling); Render's single persistent disk keeps the default deployment at one instance.
- Games and web apps run only inside the sandbox (the Preview tab and, once published, their public page): no network (no CDN assets, no fetch) and no localStorage (opaque origin), so saved scores reset on reload. Hosting them on their own domain needs Cloud Studio (Vercel token).
- Free AI providers: free tiers are rate-limited and change without notice; OpenRouter says its free models are unsuitable for production; Gemini's free tier may use prompts to improve Google's products (privacy policy must cover it, or use the paid tier); check each provider's terms for commercial use before relying on it. Quality scores for free models are yours to set: start them on the fast tier and promote to balanced only after real builds pass. Per-route caps can't express account-wide quotas (OpenRouter, Cerebras, Cloudflare), so keep one or two routes per such provider.
- The failed-build guarantee costs real AI spend on every refunded run. It is capped per workspace per day and can be switched off in Owner console → Flags; watch refunded runs against margin in Owner console → AI routing.
- Full-stack apps: the backend is declarative (collections, rules, accounts). No custom server logic, file uploads, email (verification, password reset), payments inside generated apps, roles beyond creator/signed-in/public, or relations/joins between collections yet. Sessions in the sandboxed preview and public page reset on reload (no storage in an opaque origin). They can't be deployed to Cloud Studio yet (hosting accepts websites only). End users' personal data (emails) is stored by the platform on the app creator's behalf; the privacy policy draft must cover this.
- Free app services: weather needs coordinates (no place-name search; the sandbox gives apps no device location); exchange rates are ECB daily reference rates for about 30 currencies. Both are tested against emulated upstreams only.
- Next.js websites and Expo mobile apps still have no live preview (needs a Docker host, or a Vercel token for deployed previews). Games, browser apps and full-stack apps run instantly.
- Shiprocket: prepaid orders within India only (no COD, no international, no returns/NDR handling); tracking updates on "Refresh tracking", not by webhook; delivery charges are paid from the merchant's own Shiprocket wallet and are not shown in store analytics. Extensions exist for stores only; generated websites, apps and games cannot call third-party APIs yet.
- Sandbox compute, storage, hosting and bandwidth costs are not metered yet. Margin reports say so.
- Commerce: no storefront generator, customer accounts, wishlist, reviews, returns or PayPal.
- Mobile app has no billing or owner screens yet (web only). It compiles against the new tokens (brand aliases) but has not been visually redesigned.
- Redesign items shown as **"soon"** because no backend exists yet (never faked): workspace Preview, Database, Integrations, Testing and Builds tabs; the Website Studio visual editor (canvas, component tree, properties, undo/redo, publish); Hosting and Templates; Marketing in Business Studio; app build credits and hosting usage in Billing; App Studio generation. File attachment on Create is omitted (no upload API).
- Growth: no live previews/screenshots on public pages for websites and mobile apps (needs hosting; games and browser apps are playable there); the "Made with" badge is shown on public pages but can't be injected into deployed sites until hosting exists; trending is computed per request (fine at current scale; cache it when Explore grows).
- Growth spec received only up to §3F; challenges, voting and badges remain phase G2 (comments, follows, collections are done).
